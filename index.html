📖 LUDO WARS: OFFICIAL GLOBAL RULES
🎲 1. Classic Cyber Ludo (15x15 Arena): Roll a 6 (or use Loaded Dice) to deploy your token from the Locked Base onto your colored Start Star (★). Tokens on Safe Stars (★) cannot be captured. Capturing an opponent sends them back to their Locked Base, triggers 🐂 Angry Bull Revenge, and awards you a Bonus Roll! First player to complete all 56 Steps into the Center Crown (👑) wins!
🐍 2. Cyber Snakes & Ladders (1–100 Matrix): Race from Tile 1 to Tile 100👑! Climbing any Golden/Cyan Laser Ladder grants +25 Cyber Coins & an Immediate Extra Turn. Landing on a 3D Viper Head slides your token down its coils to its tail, while landing 1 tile away triggers a 🛡️ Close-Call Dodge (+10 Coins)! Mystery Eggs (🥚) grant surprise +5 Rockets or -3 Traps.
🔥 3. Cyber Rage Stampede (1/1 Match Limit): Every player builds Rage on non-6 rolls (+6%), Snake Bites (+20%), or getting captured (+25%). At 100%, unleash a 1-Time Match Bull Stampede (+12 Steps/Tiles)!
